## The challenge

Deltatre is the world's leading sports and entertainment technology provider. In 2019, Deltatre and Massive, the company it had recently acquired, made the Childhood Eye Cancer Trust (CHECT) their charity of the year.

To raise money for the charity, they organised a five-a-side football tournament on 4 July for their own teams and a number of their corporate clients, including the Premier League, EA, the ATP and Two Circles. They wanted someone connected to the cause to present the prizes and help guests understand why CHECT's work matters.

## What I delivered

As a Patron of CHECT and someone with personal experience of retinoblastoma (Rb), the childhood eye cancer CHECT supports, the cause is personal to me. I joined the evening to present the trophies alongside CHECT's charity presentation and the raffle, and shared my story with the players and guests.

Deltatre and Massive also asked me to do a short interview on the night, to be published on their internal and possibly external channels. It covered my personal experience of Rb, the work I've done with CHECT and the support I've received from the charity, and why supporting research into Rb is so important.

## The impact

After the event, the organisers thanked me for attending and sharing my story and time with them, and said they were more than happy to show their support for CHECT.

George Hawes, VP of Delivery for Sport at Deltatre, said it was rare to hear a speaker as inspiring, and that Darren had the audience in the palm of his hand.
