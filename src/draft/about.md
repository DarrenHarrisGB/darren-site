Darren Harris built the Elite Level System the hard way — by living without it first.

Cancer survivor
He lost his sight as a child and spent years waiting for a miracle cure, instead of learning how to live without one.
Darren aged one was diagnosed with bilateral retinoblastoma, a rare form of cancer affecting both eyes. By his early twenties, he had lost his sight completely. The psychological toll was immense — anger, anxiety, and even depression. But even in the darkest moments, Darren began asking a powerful question: "Who is responsible for changing How I feel?" The answer wasn’t health care practitioners, or wider society. He decided that If you lose your sight then you unblind your mind.



Software developer



Paralympian
The turning point came during his 23-year sporting career,
as a dual Paralympian, multiple World and European medallist, and England's most-capped blind footballer.
Darren’s love of sport became a launchpad. He first rose to prominence playing blind football for England, but a setback prevented the team from competing at the Athens 2004 Paralympics. Darren pivoted to judo, a martial art he had already reached black belt, and qualified for the Beijing 2008 Paralympics. He later returned to football, competing at the London 2012 Paralympics, and went on to become England’s most capped blind footballer, with 157 appearances and 10 World and European medals.
