The Defining Moment Your People Can't Afford to Get Wrong

Every role has one - the pitch, the crisis call, the crunch meeting - a moment when the true nature, strengths, or weaknesses of a person or group become clear.



People don't lose that moment because they don't know what to do. They lose it because, under pressure, they can't access what they already know. That's the mental skills gap: the space between knowing and doing.

About Darren

Darren Harris built the Elite Level System the hard way — by living without it first.



He lost his sight as a child and spent years waiting for a miracle cure, instead of learning how to live without one. The turning point came during his 23-year sporting career, when he realised the problem was never a lack of talent or effort but a missing set of mental skills. He went from psychology sceptic to scholar, closing the gap over that period as a dual Paralympian, multiple World and European medallist, and England's most-capped blind footballer.
