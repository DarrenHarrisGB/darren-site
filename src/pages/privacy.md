# Privacy Policy

## Who I am

This website, darrenharrisgb.com, is run by Darren Harris, a sole trader based in Sutton Coldfield, UK. I'm the data controller for any personal information you share through this site, which means I decide how it's used and I'm responsible for looking after it.

This policy covers the information you share when you visit this website, enquire about a booking, attend one of my talks, workshops or coaching sessions, take my scorecard or sign up for updates. It explains what I collect, why, who I share it with, how long I keep it and what your rights are. If you have any questions, email me at [hello@darrenharrisgb.com](mailto:hello@darrenharrisgb.com).

## What I collect and why

I only collect information you choose to give me through the forms on this site or by emailing me. I don't buy data or collect information about you from other sources.

| Where | What I collect | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booking enquiry form | Name, email, organisation, event type, date, audience size and budget | To reply to your enquiry and discuss a booking | Taking steps at your request before entering a contract |
| Mental Skills Gap Scorecard | First name, email, role and organisation (optional), and your scores | To show you your results and understand who uses the scorecard | Legitimate interests |
| Tips and newsletter emails (only if you opt in) | First name and email | To send you occasional emails with tips, news and event updates | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Talks, workshops, events and coaching | Name, email, phone number and anything you choose to share with me | To arrange and deliver the session and follow up afterwards | Contract or legitimate interests |
| Emails and calls | Your name, email address, phone number and anything you include | To reply and keep a record of our conversation | Legitimate interests |
| Bookings you confirm | Contact and billing details | To deliver the work, invoice and keep financial records | Contract and legal obligation |

Your scorecard answers are a self-reflection exercise, not a psychological assessment. I don't use them to make any automated decisions about you.

## Who I share it with

I never sell your information. I share it only with the services that help me run my business, and only as far as they need it:

- **Netlify:** hosts this website and stores the details you submit through its forms.
- **Google Workspace:** provides my email, so messages you send me are stored there.
- **Email service:** I may use an email service to send tips and newsletters, but only to people who have agreed to receive them.
- **My accountant and HMRC:** receive billing records where the law requires it.

Some of these services store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK-US Data Bridge.

## How long I keep it

- **Enquiries and scorecard results:** up to two years after we were last in touch, then deleted.
- **Tips emails:** until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Booking and financial records:** six years, as required for tax purposes.

## Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics, and I don't track your visits. My hosting provider, Netlify, may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site secure and running.

## Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as social media and organisations I work with. I'm not responsible for their content or how they handle your information, so please read their privacy policies before sharing any personal details.

## Keeping your information safe

I take sensible steps to protect your information from being lost, misused or accessed without permission, including using secure, password-protected services. However, no method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
- ask me to correct anything that's wrong
- ask me to delete your information
- object to, or ask me to limit, how I use it
- withdraw your consent to tips emails at any time
- ask me to transfer your information to someone else

To use any of these rights, email [hello@darrenharrisgb.com](mailto:hello@darrenharrisgb.com). I'll reply within one month.

## Changes to this policy

I'll update this page if anything changes. The date at the bottom shows when it was last updated.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please contact me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.
