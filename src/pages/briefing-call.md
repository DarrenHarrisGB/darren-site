# Book your briefing call

Thank you for booking me to speak at your event. This 30-minute call is where we make sure the talk fits your people, your event and the outcome you want.

## What we'll cover

- Your audience: who they are, what they're facing and what's on their minds
- The outcome you want: what people should think, feel and do differently afterwards
- Your event: the theme, running order, my slot and who speaks before and after me
- Logistics: venue, timings, AV, microphone and stage access
- Your contact on the day, and how you'd like me to be introduced

## How to get the most from the call

It helps if you can share any of the following beforehand, or have them to hand on the call:

- The event agenda or running order
- Any key messages, values or challenges you'd like the talk to connect with
- Words and phrases your organisation uses, so the talk sounds like it was built for you

Don't worry if you haven't got everything yet. We can fill in the gaps together.
