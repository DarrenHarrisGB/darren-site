An awards ceremony has two audiences. There are the students walking up to collect a prize, and there's everyone else in the hall: the ones who worked just as hard and go home empty-handed, the parents who give up an evening, the staff who make it all happen. A great guest speaker speaks to all of them. That's what I do.

## This isn't another sports talk

**Get a story about what it really takes to achieve anything**

I'm a two-sport Paralympian, England's most-capped blind footballer, and ten-time World and European medallist with a degree in maths, masters in psychology, and honorary doctorate for outstanding public service.
“We've had so much feedback from both parents and children, on how much they enjoyed listening to Darren and how fun he made the evening.”
St. Dominic's Priory School

I've attended hundreds of awards ceremonies and won dozens of awards throughout my life. The person presenting the awards matters almost as much as the pupil receiving it. With a member of staff alongside me on stage, I can shake hands, hand over the prize, and if required, pose for photographs.

## The message aligns with your school values

**Send every student home believing their effort counts.**

Awards celebrate results. My message is about what produces them: the everyday habits, the preparation nobody sees, and how you respond when things go wrong. Winners leave knowing why they succeeded. Everyone else leaves knowing exactly what to do next.

“It was evident that students and staff alike were motivated and inspired having listened to your speech.”

I’m a multiple award-winning speaker and two-sport Paralympian with a Master’s in Psychology.
