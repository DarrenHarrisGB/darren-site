## Which challenge are you facing?

<div class="accordion">
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-header">Our staff struggle to include pupils with SEND</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>Having learned in both mainstream and special education, I’ve seen the strengths and shortcomings of each. Mainstream classrooms offer a broad curriculum but rigid pacing, while specialist schools provide tailored support but with isolation from peers. I show staff how to bring the best of both into their classrooms, so every student belongs and thrives.</p>
      <p><strong>Staff leave knowing:</strong> inclusion starts with high expectations, and small changes in how they teach can remove big barriers.</p>
      <blockquote>
        <p>&ldquo;The brief was to deliver a talk to a conference with senior leaders from the FE College sector on how our institutions can be inclusive for people from a SEND background. Darren shared his personal experiences and barriers which were incredibly emotive and landed with those in attendance.&rdquo;</p>
        <footer>&mdash; <strong>Aaron Caisley</strong>, Associate Director, <cite>West Herts College</cite></footer>
      </blockquote>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-header">Pressure on staff is affecting how they communicate</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>As an England blind footballer, I couldn't see my teammates, so clear communication was the only way we could play. I show staff how the way they speak to pupils, parents and each other affects everyone's wellbeing, and give them simple techniques to communicate clearly and calmly, even under pressure.</p>
      <p><strong>Staff leave knowing:</strong> good communication protects wellbeing, theirs and their pupils'.</p>
      <blockquote>
        <p>&ldquo;Feedback from teachers was overwhelmingly positive, with many highlighting how relevant Darren made his message.&rdquo;</p>
        <footer>&mdash; <strong>Rebecca Lillington</strong>, Director of Professional Learning, <cite>Summit Learning Trust</cite></footer>
      </blockquote>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-header">We want every adult in school to lead</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>In blind football, the goalkeeper, the guide behind the goal and the coach all direct players by voice, so leadership comes from everyone, not just the captain. I show staff that leadership isn’t down to title or tenure, and that every adult in school leads through the way they communicate.</p>
      <p><strong>Staff leave knowing:</strong> everyone leads, whatever their role.</p>
      <blockquote>
        <p>&ldquo;Making our headteachers more conscious of how they communicate was an added benefit we didn&rsquo;t expect.&rdquo;</p>
        <footer>&mdash; <strong>Matthew Crawford</strong>, Chief Executive Officer, <cite>Embark Federation</cite></footer>
      </blockquote>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">

    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>As a two-sport Paralympian with a Master’s in Psychology, I show staff how physical activity improves pupils’ mental health, behaviour and attainment, and how to build more movement into the school day without losing learning time.</p>
      <p><strong>Staff leave knowing:</strong> active pupils are better learners.</p>
      <blockquote>
        <p>&ldquo;Our teachers left the session with a better understanding of how physical activity improves academic performance.&rdquo;</p>

## About Darren Harris

I’m a multiple award-winning speaker and two-sport Paralympian with a Master’s in Psychology. As a lifelong learner, school governor, and proud father, my talks engage students, teachers and parents alike. Since 2013, I’ve spoken to over 100,000 students in primary, secondary, FE colleges and universities across the UK and around the world.
