# Information For Event Planners

The text and images below have been provided to help you plan and organise your event. If you want customised assets, like a promo video, just ask. If you have any additional requests, I aim to be as easy as possible to work with before, during, and after our time together.

## Speaker Bio
This is a short description that you can use to market Darren for your event:
> Darren Harris built his **elite performance mindset** the hard way -  by living without it first. He lost his sight as a child and spent years waiting for a miracle cure, instead of learning how to live without one.
>
> The turning point came during his 23-year sporting career, where he became a two-sport Paralympian, multiple World and European medallist, and most-capped England blind footballer.
>
> During this period, he went from psychology sceptic to scholar, when he realised the problem was never a lack of talent or effort but a missing set of mental skills.
>
> As an award-winning keynote speaker, he specialises in giving leaders and teams the psychological tools to better prepare for and respond to adversity, and has delivered 450+ presentations to 150,000+ people worldwide since 2013.
>
> Beyond the stage, Darren sits on various boards and committees, plays the piano and guitar, and loves an early morning run.

## Speaker Intro
This is a short introduction that the MC reads aloud to welcome Darren Harris to the stage:

>

> Please help me welcome to the stage, Darren Harris.

## Speaker AV Request
