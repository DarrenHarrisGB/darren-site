# The Elite Performance Mindset
This is both a mindset I lived as an elite athlete and the framework I now teach. It comprises three steps:

<div class="accordion" id="process-accordion">
  <details class="accordion-item" open>
    <summary class="accordion-header">1️⃣ The Signs</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>Here are three signs of under-performance:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Performance ceiling</strong>: You&rsquo;ve plateaued rather than peaked</li>
        <li><strong>Performance inconsistency</strong>: You&rsquo;re good one day, average the next</li>
        <li><strong>Performance slump</strong>: You&rsquo;ve dropped off, gradually or suddenly</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-header">2️⃣ The Causes</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>Here are three models of disability:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Medical model</strong>: You are disabled by disease</li>
        <li><strong>Social model</strong>: You are disabled by society</li>
        <li><strong>Psychological model</strong>: You are disabled by behaviour</li>
      </ul>
      <p>The first model asks what&rsquo;s wrong with you, the second asks what&rsquo;s wrong with the world, but the third asks what you can do about it.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item">
    <summary class="accordion-header">3️⃣ The Skills</summary>
    <div class="accordion-panel">
      <p>There are three levels of performance:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Cultivate</strong>: Your <em>every day</em> skills</li>
        <li><strong>Prepare</strong>: Your <em>before performance</em> skills</li>
        <li><strong>Respond</strong>: Your <em>during performance</em> skills</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Each level has specific mental skills.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>
