## Programme description

Most employee resource groups start with real energy: a launch event, a full room and a long list of ideas. Then the day job gets in the way. A handful of volunteers end up doing everything, members go quiet and senior sponsors drift away. When the chair moves on, the network often fades with them. The cause still matters, but the network loses its voice.

For almost a decade, Darren worked as a software developer, first with British Steel and then Capgemini, as the only blind person in the company. That was before ERGs existed, so there was no network to turn to, and it gave him a lasting interest in how organisations support people from under-represented groups. Today he serves as a non-executive director and school governor. In this session, he shows ERG leaders how to grow a network that lasts, with a clear purpose, engaged members, committed senior backing and the influence to shape recruitment, retention and progression across the organisation.

## Speaking at your network's events

Darren also speaks at ERG events throughout the year, including National Inclusion Week, Black History Month and men's mental health days. Every talk is tailored to your theme and audience, helping you start honest conversations and give members a reason to get involved.

## This programme is perfect for
- ERG chairs, co-chairs and committee members
- Executive sponsors who want their networks to have real influence
- D&I and HR teams supporting several staff networks

## The audience will leave with
- A clear purpose for their network that the business understands and values
- Practical ways to turn silent members into active ones and share the workload
- Ways to use their network's voice to improve recruitment, retention and progression
- A plan for succession, senior sponsorship and showing the network's impact
