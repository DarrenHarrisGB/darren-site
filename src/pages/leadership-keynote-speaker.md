## Programme description
Every leader has a favourite position. Some stay out in front, setting the pace and making every call. Others stand beside their team and struggle to make the tough decision. The rest step so far back their people feel left alone. Each position works brilliantly in the right moment and badly in the wrong one. Under pressure, most leaders fall back on whichever feels comfortable, whether the situation calls for it or not. That's when their teams lose focus, confidence, and trust.

Darren captained England's blind football team, and serves as a non-executive director and school governor. In this session, he shows that each position needs its own mental skills, and he gives leaders practical techniques to read the moment, switch position and keep their team on track.

## This programme is perfect for
- Senior leaders and managers making high-stakes decisions
- New or emerging leaders stepping up to lead their former peers
- Leadership teams going through change, growth or a period of uncertainty

## The audience will leave with
- A clear picture of their default leadership position, and the moments when it lets their team down
- The mental skills needed for each position
- Practical techniques to read the situation and switch positions when required
