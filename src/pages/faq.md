Why become an Adversity Alchemist

Every leader, team, and organisation faces adversity. It is unavoidable. But while challenges can feel isolating, and overwhelming, they also hold the potential for growth, resilience, and transformation.



Darren Harris has experienced this first hand. As a Paralympian and psychologist, he is the perfect person to give leaders and teams with the tools to turn adversity into advantage.



His Unblind Your Mind programme includes keynote talks, interactive workshops and executive coaching that equip audiences with the mindset and strategies to succeed because of their challenges, not despite them.
