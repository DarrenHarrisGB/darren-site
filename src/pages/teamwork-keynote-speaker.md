## Programme description

Nearly every team has defined roles. Very few have clear boundaries. Where responsibilities overlap, either two people chase the same ball or both leave it to the other. Effort gets duplicated, tasks get dropped and fingers get pointed. Under pressure, these grey areas are where talented teams come apart.

Darren is England's most-capped blind footballer and a former England captain. In blind football, the outfield players can't see the ball or each other, so the space between players is where the game is won or lost. Players and guides talk constantly so everyone knows who's in possession of the ball. Remote and hybrid teams face the same challenge: when you can't see what your colleagues are doing, you have to communicate more. In this session, Darren shows teams how to communicate in their own shared spaces, so nothing gets duplicated, nothing gets dropped and nobody gets blamed.

## This programme is perfect for
- Teams where roles overlap or responsibilities are unclear
- Remote and hybrid teams who rarely see each other in person
- New, growing or restructured teams
- Cross-functional and project teams working across departments

## The audience will leave with
- A clear picture of where their roles overlap and where things get dropped
- Simple ways to claim a task, hand it over or leave it, so everyone knows who has the ball
- Communication habits that build trust and stop the blame game
