## About Darren Harris

I’m a multiple award-winning speaker and two-sport Paralympian with a Master’s in Psychology. As a lifelong learner, school governor, and proud father, my talks engage students, teachers and parents alike. Since 2013, I’ve spoken to over 100,000 students in primary, secondary, FE colleges and universities across the UK and around the world.
